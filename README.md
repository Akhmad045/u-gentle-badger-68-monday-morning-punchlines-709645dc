`main.py` writes a single joke about Monday mornings to `/output/entry.txt`:

**My bed filed for custody of me on Monday morning. The judge sided with my alarm.**

A custody battle between the bed and the alarm, decided on a Monday
morning — a concrete, absurd image with a twist ending (the alarm wins,
for once), told in 80 characters with no wasted setup and no tired
Monday clichés.
